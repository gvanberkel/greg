# Table Mountain Cableway, City Sightseeing Southern Africa & Morii

*What are these companies?:* **Table Mountain Cableway and City Sightseeing are two of Cape Town's most prominent tourism brands.**

In 2021 I worked on proposals for both Table Mountain Cableway and City Sightseeing Southern Africa and after a prolonged assessment period Code Collective was fortuitously selected for both. On winning the accounts, I set out, with my team, to create a valuable product development trajectory and to establish a modern, fit for purpose software architecture.

Today Code Collective are responsible for the software that runs all operational and customer facing aspects of the City Sightseeing business and for the software that runs some of Table Mountain Cableways data analysis, operations and ticket processing.
