# Guidepost

*What was Guidepost:* **Was (past tense) a** ***diabetes care technology company***

In 2011 I helped Guidepost establish a development team (within Code Collective) to build their remote diabetes care platform. From Jan 2022 until 2026 (when the business sold its technology assets and shut down) I served as their CTO. I was responsible for supporting technology development in the business with sound implementation strategies.

In 2026 - Code Collective extended their involvement by establishing partner development teams for 2 of the companies that purchased technology IP from Guidepost.

At its peak Guidepost was valued at several hundred million (ZAR) and served thousands of people with diabetes.
